01 / ABOUT MIRA

A voice of her own.
A world of her own.

Mira Vellani is a virtual AI artist.

Her musical world brings together Italo Disco, 80s synthpop and Eurodance. Between the nostalgic glow of synthesizers and Mediterranean evenings, the music takes centre stage.

This wiki collects her music and the confirmed information about it. Mira is an artistic identity created with AI; this portrayal does not describe a real singer.
